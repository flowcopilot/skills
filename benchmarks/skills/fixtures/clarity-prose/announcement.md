# Project export beta

We are absolutely thrilled to announce a transformative new chapter in the way that our customers can work with their information. The project export beta opens on November 2, 2026. It is available to workspace administrators on the Team plan. Each export includes projects and comments in a ZIP file. The download link expires after 24 hours. Exports do not include attachments. During the beta, each workspace can create three exports per day. There is no extra charge for beta access. To join, administrators must enable Project exports in Settings before November 16, 2026. Send feedback to exports@example.test. This milestone reflects our enduring commitment to empowering teams with a more seamless and intuitive workflow, and we look forward to the valuable insights that this exciting journey will bring.
